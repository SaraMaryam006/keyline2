Everything a landlord and a tenant needs

We handle the daily work of renting a property, from finding the right tenant to returning the deposit.

Rent collection

Online payments by UPI, card or bank transfer, automatic reminders and a monthly statement for owners.

Lease management

Digital agreements, renewals and escalation clauses tracked, with alerts 60 days before expiry.

Tenant screening

Identity, employment and rental-history checks so you only lease to reliable tenants.

Maintenance requests

Tenants log a repair with a photo. Our verified vendors are assigned and the status is updated live.

Owner reports

Income, expenses, occupancy and tax-ready summaries in a dashboard, available whenever you log in.

Inspections and compliance

Move-in and move-out inspections with photos, plus safety checks that keep you compliant.

Housing with a purpose

Keyline works with Asha Bhavan Trust, a community NGO, to place families in safe, affordable homes. The same system that manages our market rentals also tracks each partner family’s lease, concessional rent and repairs.

  • Concessional rents for families referred by the NGO.
  • Priority repairs so no family waits for a basic fix.
  • Transparent reporting to the NGO on families housed and rent collected.
